Sialkot Plant: 6-Stage Tour

Walk the precise production journey from digitized CAD patterns to solar-stamped international export containers.

STAGE 01 OF 06 LEVEL 2 · ENGINEERING DECK

CAD & Technical Patterning Room

Every custom OEM run begins with sub-millimeter digital grading and Gerber computerized nesting markers. We convert buyer tech-packs directly into nested patterns that maximize fabric utilization up to 94.2%, reducing material waste and carbon load before fabric is even unrolled.

Pattern Tolerance ± 0.5 mm Laser calibration alignment
Nesting Software Gerber / OptiTex Direct DXF/AAMA import
Viewport 01: Vector Pattern Staging & Prototype Grading
STAGE 02 OF 06 LEVEL 1 · CUTTING BAYS

Automated Fabric Laser Cutting Floor

Using zero-burn multi-ply automated textile cutters and high-frequency laser tables, 1000D Cordura, X-Pac laminates, and ripstop tarpaulins are sealed at the edge instantaneously. Edge fraying is completely eliminated, ensuring high seam burst resistance under extreme loads.

Cutting Mode Laser & CNC Blade Automated vacuum-bed hold
Edge Seal Quality 100% Cauterized Zero fraying on 1000D
Viewport 02: Multi-Ply CNC Cutting Table & Automated Vacuum Hold
STAGE 03 OF 06 LEVEL 2 · ASSEMBLY LINES

Heavy Stitching & Programmable Assembly Lines

The heart of Sialkot craftsmanship: synchronized heavy-duty walking foot machines running heavy nylon bonded V-69 and V-92 threads. Critical load anchors use computerized programmable pattern stitchers (X-box bar-tacks) to lock shoulder straps to 120kg breaking tensions.

Thread Spec Bonded Nylon V-69 High UV & rot resistant
Bar-Tack Density 42 Stitches / in Reinforced stress anchors
Viewport 03: Synchronized Walking Foot Sewing Stations
STAGE 04 OF 06 LEVEL 3 · HARDWARE ANCHORS

Hardware Fixtures & Reinforced Riveting

Acetal Duraflex buckles, Fidlock magnetic slide mounts, YKK Aquaguard inverted coil zippers, and aerospace-grade matte black alloy D-rings are installed with calibrated pneumatic presses to avoid stress fractures and ensure flawless cold-weather snap action.

Component Grades Mil-Spec Acetal Cold rated down to -30°C
Fastener Press Pneumatic Calibrated Consistent Newton strike
Viewport 04: Pneumatic Hardware Insertion & Sub-Assembly Fixtures
STAGE 05 OF 06 LEVEL 3 · CERTIFICATION LAB

Hydrostatic Testing & Pull Gauge Lab

Every production lot undergoes Suter Hydrostatic Head water pressure testing (up to 10,000mm H2O), seam pull-burst stress cycles on a digital tensiometer, and 10,000-cycle reciprocal zipper endurance tests to ensure uncompromising field reliability.

Hydrostatic Rating 10,000 mm H₂O Sealed seam verification
Zipper Cycle Stress 10,000+ Runs Continuous robotic tester
Viewport 05: Hydrostatic Head Chamber & Seam Burst Sensor
STAGE 06 OF 06 LEVEL 1 · EXPORT STAGING

Vacuum Finishing & Climate-Controlled Crating

Final finished gear is hot-air thread clipped, vacuum suction cleaned, packed with food-grade silica desiccant pouches, and wrapped in 100% recycled LDPE protective liners. Export master cartons are palletized under ISO weather wrap for maritime or air freight.

Humidity Control < 45% RH Automated sensor logging
Carton Traceability 2D Barcode Serial Pallet-to-container tracking
Viewport 06: Vacuum Thread Finishing & Palletized Export Bay
